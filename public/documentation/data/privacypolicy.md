# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 May 2026

TL;DR its safe bro

## what i collect

On the homepage only, I use Umami Analytics to count page views. That's it.
No cookies. No fingerprinting. No personal data. Just anonymous numbers like
"100 people visited today" and "most came from Google."

Inside the app (/app/) — nothing. Zero. Your notes are yours, I don't look at them,
I don't analyze them, I don't sell them.

## What I don't collect

- Your name
- Your email
- Your location
- Your notes or any content you write
- Anything that could identify you personally

## Third parties

The only third party is Umami Analytics on the homepage. It's open source and
privacy-first. You can read about it at umami.is.

No Google. No Facebook. No ad networks (till now)

## Your data

You can delete the IndexedDB to nuke your notes

## Contact

Got a question or concern? Open an issue on GitHub or send a message on telegram @ivaansri.

---

That's genuinely it. No hidden clauses. No lawyer speak. Just a note-taking app
that respects you.
